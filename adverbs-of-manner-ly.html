1What are adverbs of manner? 🏃

An adverb of manner tells us HOW something is done: quickly, carefully, happily. Most are made from an adjective + -ly. In Spanish, the equivalent is the ending -mente.

AdjectiveAdverb (-ly)Spanish
quickquicklyrápidamente
slowslowlylentamente
carefulcarefullycuidadosamente
happyhappilyfelizmente

Key ideas

  • Adjective → describes a noun: a quick runner.
  • Adverb → describes a verb: she runs quickly.
  • Answer the question “How?”: How does she sing? Beautifully.
  • Spanish -mente ≈ English -ly.
She speaks slowly. Habla despacio.
He drives carefully. Conduce con cuidado.
The children played happily. Los niños jugaron felices.
Please listen carefully. Por favor, escucha con atención.

2Making -ly adverbs ✍️

Most adverbs just add -ly, but some adjectives change their spelling. Tap a card to listen.

Just add -ly

-y → -ily

-le → -ly (drop the e)

-ic → -ically

Special spellings

📌 Careful: -ful → -fully (two l’s): careful → carefully. And true → truly (no e!).
3Irregular and tricky adverbs ⚠️

Some adverbs do not end in -ly. Others change meaning when you add -ly.

AdjectiveAdverbExample
goodwellShe sings well.
fastfastHe drives fast.
hardhardThey work hard.
latelateWe arrived late.
earlyearlyI get up early.
straightstraightGo straight on.

Same word, different meaning

Without -lyWith -lySpanish
hard = a lot of efforthardly = almost notduro / apenas
late = not on timelately = recentlytarde / últimamente
near = closenearly = almostcerca / casi
high = up highhighly = veryalto / muy
He works hard. Trabaja duro.
He hardly works. Apenas trabaja.
I arrived late. Llegué tarde.
I haven’t seen her lately. No la he visto últimamente.
The glass is nearly full. El vaso está casi lleno.
⚠️ Never say hardly work for “work hard”, or lately for “late”.
4Adjectives that end in -ly 🙃

Not every word ending in -ly is an adverb. These are adjectives: they describe nouns.

Adjectives in -ly

She is very friendly. Es muy amable.
It was a lovely day. Fue un día precioso.

How to describe an action?

She spoke in a friendly way. Habló de manera amable.
He smiled in a friendly way. Sonrió amablemente.
📌 There is no adverb friendly-ly. Use in a friendly way.
5Where do they go? 📍

The most natural place is after the verb, or after the object if there is one. Never put it between the verb and the object.

Subject + verb + (object) + adverb
PositionExampleNotes
After the verbShe sings beautifully. No object
After the objectHe opened the door slowly. Most common
Before the main verbShe carefully read the text. Emphasis, a bit formal
At the startSlowly, he opened the door. Dramatic, stories

Spanish speakers: be careful!

❌ Wrong✅ Correct
She speaks fluently English.She speaks English fluently.
He ate quickly his lunch.He ate his lunch quickly.
📌 In Spanish you can say abrió lentamente la puerta. In English: opened the door slowly.
6Adjective or adverb? 🔍

Use an adjective to describe a noun, and an adverb to describe an action.

Adjective (noun)

  • a quick answer
  • a careful driver
  • a good singer
  • She looks happy.

Adverb (verb)

  • answer quickly
  • drives carefully
  • sings well
  • She smiles happily.
He is a careful driver. He drives carefully. Es un conductor cuidadoso. Conduce con cuidado.
She is a good singer. She sings well. Es una buena cantante. Canta bien.
The soup tastes delicious. La sopa sabe deliciosa.
She looks tired. Parece cansada.
⚠️ After be, look, feel, seem, taste, smell, sound use an adjective: She looks sad (not sadly).
7Comparing adverbs 📈

We compare adverbs like adjectives: -er / -est for short ones and more / most for -ly adverbs.

AdverbComparativeSuperlative
fastfasterthe fastest
hardharderthe hardest
earlyearlierthe earliest
quicklymore quicklythe most quickly
carefullymore carefullythe most carefully
wellbetterthe best
badlyworsethe worst
He runs faster than me. Corre más rápido que yo.
She drives more carefully than her dad. Conduce con más cuidado que su padre.
She sings the best in the class. Es la que mejor canta de la clase.
Run as quickly as you can. Corre tan rápido como puedas.
⚠️ Never say more quick: it is more quickly or quicker.

10Cheat sheet & memory tricks 🧠

✅ Do

  • Use adjective + -ly for “how”
  • Change -y → -ily: happily
  • Say well (not good) for actions
  • Put the adverb after the object
  • Use more + -ly adverb to compare

❌ Don’t

  • Don’t say speaks good
  • Don’t say fluently English
  • Don’t mix hard / hardly
  • Don’t mix late / lately
  • Don’t say looks sadly

Memory tricks

  • 🇪🇸 -mente = -ly. rápidamente → quickly.
  • ❓ “How?” → adverb of manner.
  • 🎯 Verb + object + adverb. Say it in that order.
  • 🗣️ Say new phrases aloud with the 🔊 buttons.
