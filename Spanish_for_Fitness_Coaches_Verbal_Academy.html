How to use it: tap 🔊 to hear Latin American Spanish. For speaking practice use Chrome, Edge or Safari and allow the microphone.
Explanation: every Spanish noun has a gender: el (masculine) or la (feminine). Words ending in -miento are masculine (el entrenamiento, el estiramiento); words ending in -ción are feminine (la hidratación). Most words in -a are feminine (la dieta, la meta, la plancha, la sentadilla). Las pesas is always plural. Stress: mús-cu-lo, pro-te-í-na, hi-dra-ta-ción.

Explanation: for instructions use the formal command (usted): Mantenga, Inspire, Descanse, Repita, Beba. Use Asegúrese de + infinitive for "make sure you…". Ask about sensations with ¿Cómo se siente?. Repetición = rep, serie = set. Many gyms use tú: stay with usted unless the client invites you to use tú.
